# HMM-based modification enzyme annotation

## Summary

Uses HMMer profile hidden Markov models to detect RiPP modification enzymes (e.g. lanthionine synthetases, lasso cyclases, cyanobactin proteases) in translated genome sequences, anchoring the construction of putative biosynthetic gene cluster windows for RiPP precursor mining in MetaMiner.

## When to use

Use when mining nucleotide genomes (FASTA contigs or assemblies) for RiPP-encoding BGCs and you need to locate modification enzyme homologs to define candidate precursor peptide regions before spectra matching.

## When NOT to use

- When the goal is exhaustive precursor enumeration regardless of enzyme homology — all-ORF mode (6-frame translation + <200 aa ORF threshold) is the alternative, at the cost of ~4 orders of magnitude more candidates
- When peptides of interest lack sequence similarity to known RiPP modification enzymes — the motif-ORF strategy misses them, so HMM anchoring is inappropriate
- When the RiPP class has no curated HMM profile available; the enzyme-based windowing cannot be performed

## Inputs

- Nucleotide FASTA genome assemblies or assembled metagenomic contigs
- HMMer profile HMMs for RiPP modification enzyme families (lanthipeptide, lassopeptide, LAP, linaridin, glycocin, cyanobactin, proteusin classes)

## Outputs

- Modification enzyme hit coordinates per genome (HMMer sequence hits)
- 10 kbp putative biosynthetic gene cluster windows centered on each enzyme hit
- Candidate short ORF precursor peptides from motif-ORF mode within those windows

## How to apply

Translate the input DNA sequence into protein sequence via 6-frame translation, then run HMMer (Eddy, 2011) on the resulting protein sequences to detect all RiPP modification enzymes against class-specific HMM profiles. For each detected enzyme hit, construct a 10 kbp window centered on the hit — MetaMiner calls this window the putative biosynthetic gene cluster — and screen ORFs inside it as candidate precursor peptides (motif-ORF strategy). This enzyme-anchored strategy drastically reduces the candidate space: on the 36 ACTI strains the motif-ORF mode yielded only 67 short ORF candidates versus 565,138 in all-ORF mode (default threshold: ORFs shorter than 200 amino acids). The candidate precursor peptides (plus decoys generated by random shuffling) are then searched against spectra, with p-values from MS-DPR and FDR estimated by the target-decoy approach at a 1% threshold. Choose HMM profiles matching the RiPP classes supported by MetaMiner: lanthipeptides, lassopeptides, LAPs, linaridins, glycocins, cyanobactins, and proteusins.

## Related tools

- **HMMer** (Runs profile HMM searches on 6-frame-translated sequences to detect all RiPP modification enzymes that anchor putative BGC windows)
- **MetaMiner** (Orchestrates the motif-ORF pipeline: builds 10 kbp windows around HMMer-detected enzymes and extracts candidate precursor ORFs) — https://github.com/mohimanilab/MetaMiner

## Examples

```
hmmsearch --cpu 8 lan_monomer.hmm sixframe_translated_proteins.fasta
```

## Evaluation signals

- On the 36 ACTI RefSeq genomes, aggregate motif-ORF short ORF counts should equal 67 (with 168 RiPP-encoding clusters among 1,140 antiSMASH BGCs), vs 565,138 for all-ORF mode — a ~4-order-of-magnitude candidate reduction
- For Streptomyces roseosporus NRRL 11379, expect 2 motif-ORF vs 14,694 all-ORF short ORFs
- Every putative BGC window must be exactly 10 kbp and centered on an HMMer enzyme hit
- Positive control: MetaMiner's full pipeline (anchored by enzyme detection) identified all 18 known RiPPs in the positive control dataset
- Downstream identifications must pass 1% FDR estimated via the target-decoy (randomly shuffled peptide) approach

## Limitations

- The motif-ORF strategy is not sensitive: peptides whose enzymes have no sequence similarity to known RiPP modification enzyme profiles are missed
- Only RiPP classes with curated HMMs (lanthipeptides, lassopeptides, LAPs, linaridins, glycocins, cyanobactins, proteusins) can be anchored; other BGC classes require antiSMASH or other methods
- 10 kbp windows may miss precursor peptides located outside the window around the modification enzyme
