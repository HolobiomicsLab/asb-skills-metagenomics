# protein-secondary-structure-prediction-evaluation

## Summary

Benchmark the secondary-structure (and solvent-accessible surface area) accuracy of a multitask deep network, trained jointly with contact prediction from MSAs, against published methods (RaptorX-Property and SCRATCH-1D) on CASP11 (105 domains) and CASP12 (55 domains).

## When to use

Use when a protein secondary-structure/ASA predictor must be benchmarked against reference methods (e.g. RaptorX-Property, SCRATCH-1D) on CASP11/CASP12 target domains, or when training a multitask network that couples 2D contact feature maps with 1D structure predictions.

## When NOT to use

- When only tertiary-structure/contact benchmarking is needed and no secondary-structure or ASA accuracy comparison is required
- When the comparison methods (RaptorX-Property, SCRATCH-1D) cannot be run or their predictions retrieved (e.g. server errors), making a fair three-way comparison impossible

## Inputs

- Amino-acid sequences in FASTA format (placed in ./dataset/fasta)
- MSAs generated by HHblits against the UniProt20_2016 library
- Pre-trained multitask models and training datasets from the DeepECA Google Drive folder
- CASP11 (105 domains) and CASP12 (55 domains) target-domain datasets for benchmarking

## Outputs

- Contact predictions stored in ./contact_pred
- Secondary-structure predictions stored in ./ss_pred
- Secondary-structure accuracy comparisons (Tables 4-5) and ASA accuracy comparisons (Table 6) versus RaptorX-Property and SCRATCH-1D

## How to apply

Download the pre-trained multitask models and training datasets from the DeepECA Google Drive folder, generate MSAs with HHblits (three iterations, E-value 0.001, 60% coverage, UniProt20_2016 library) after setting the hhblits path in run_hhblits_local.py. Train the network via multitask.py: a shared 20-residual-block trunk with task-specific 10-block heads, ADAM optimizer at learning rate 0.0005, batch normalization, one domain per batch, 200-residue cropping, and sampling up to 30,000 MSA sequences (2-3 days on an NVIDIA TITAN GTX GPU). Run pipeline.py to produce predictions in ./contact_pred and ./ss_pred, optionally placing custom FASTA files in ./dataset/fasta. Evaluate on CASP11 (105 domains) and CASP12 (55 domains), comparing secondary-structure accuracy (Tables 4-5) and ASA accuracy (Table 6) against RaptorX-Property and SCRATCH-1D; the rationale is that the 2D contact feature maps provide a good representation for secondary-structure prediction.

## Related tools

- **DeepECA** (Multitask DNN (shared 20-residual-block trunk plus task-specific heads) used to predict secondary structure, ASA, and contacts; evaluation target of this skill) — https://github.com/tomiilab/DeepECA
- **HHblits** (Generates the MSAs (three iterations, E-value 0.001, 60% coverage, UniProt20_2016) consumed by the multitask model)

## Examples

```
python pipeline.py
```

## Evaluation signals

- The multitask model outperforms RaptorX-Property and SCRATCH-1D on secondary-structure prediction accuracy on both CASP11 and CASP12
- The multitask model achieves the highest ASA prediction accuracy among the three methods on CASP11 and CASP12 (Table 6)
- Prediction result files are present in ./contact_pred and ./ss_pred after running pipeline.py
- Secondary-structure accuracy is computed on the full CASP11 (105 domains) and CASP12 (55 domains) benchmark sets
- Training reproduces the stated configuration: ADAM, lr 0.0005, batch normalization, one domain per batch, 200-residue cropping, up to 30,000 sampled MSA sequences

## Limitations

- GPU memory limitation constrained the network depth to 60 layers
- RaptorX-Contact comparison results were missing for 3 of 105 CASP11 domains and 1 of 55 CASP12 domains due to server errors, and RaptorX-Contact has no local execution file
- Training takes 2-3 days on an NVIDIA TITAN GTX GPU, requiring setup of the HHblits database and hhblits path in run_hhblits_local.py
- Pre-trained models and datasets must be downloaded from an external Google Drive folder before any prediction or training
